1. Threshold for Splitting Populous Polling Units

A polling unit shall be split into sub-units only when the number of registered voters exceeds 1,000 voters.

2. Notice Period for Relocated Polling Units

Notice of any polling unit relocated from a private residence or worship centre must be published at least 7 days before the election.3. Climate and Flood Emergency Relocation

Where flash flooding submerges a polling unit, voting may be relocated to any nearby dry building outside the registration area.

4. Geo-Coordinates Registry

Electoral Officers shall verify GPS coordinates for all newly split polling units.
