1. Threshold for Splitting Populous Polling Units

A polling unit shall be split into voting points or new polling units when the number of registered voters exceeds 750 voters.

2. Notice Period for Relocated Polling Units

Notice of any polling unit relocated from a private residence or worship centre must be published at least 30 days before the election.3. Climate and Flood Emergency Relocation

Where flash flooding submerges a polling unit, voting must be relocated strictly to a designated public school or civic hall within the same registration area.

4. Geo-Coordinates Registry

Electoral Officers shall verify and publish GPS coordinates for all newly split polling units.
