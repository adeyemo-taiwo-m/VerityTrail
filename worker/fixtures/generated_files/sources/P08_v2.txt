1. Public Posting of Form EC8A at Polling Unit

After counting votes, the Presiding Officer must mandatorily paste the duplicate Form EC8A EC60E poster conspicuously at the polling unit before leaving for the ward collation centre.

2. Electronic Transmission of Result Sheets

The Presiding Officer shall scan and transmit the Form EC8A result sheet directly from the polling unit immediately after signing and announcing the scores.3. Statutory Definition of Over-Voting

Over-voting occurs where total votes cast at a polling unit exceed the total number of accredited voters recorded on the BVAS device for that polling unit.

4. Packaging of Tamper-Evident Envelopes

Used ballot counterfoils and result copies shall be sealed inside tamper-evident envelope EC50A before departure.
