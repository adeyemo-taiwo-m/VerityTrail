1. Timeline for Transfer of Registered Voters

Applications for transfer of voter registration must be submitted not later than 30 days before election day.

2. Display of Preliminary Register for Claims and Objections

The preliminary register of voters shall be displayed at ward headquarters for 7 days for public scrutiny.3. Replacement of Lost or Damaged PVC

An application for replacement of a lost Permanent Voter's Card must include a sworn court affidavit and a police extract.

4. Archiving of Objections

Revision officers shall file daily summaries of claims received at the ward centre.
