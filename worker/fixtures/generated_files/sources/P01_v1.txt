1. Continuous Voter Registration

Eligible citizens may register to vote at any registration centre until 31 December 2026.

2. Eligibility

A person must be at least 18 years old and a citizen of Nigeria to register.3. Collection of PVC

Registered voters must collect their Permanent Voter's Card within 30 days of notification.

4. Accreditation

Voters must present their PVC for accreditation before voting.

Voters may be accompanied by one companion while waiting.
