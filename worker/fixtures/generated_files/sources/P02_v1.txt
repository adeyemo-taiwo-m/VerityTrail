1. Polling Unit Opening and Closing Hours

Accreditation and voting at all polling units shall close promptly at 2:00 PM.

2. Biometric Authentication on BVAS

Where fingerprint authentication fails on the BVAS device, the Presiding Officer shall issue an Incident Form to the voter.3. Voter Registration Eligibility

To register as a voter, an applicant must be at least 18 years old and provide proof of 12 months residency in the ward.

4. Presiding Officer Logbook

The Presiding Officer shall record the serial number of the BVAS device before opening the poll.
