1. Biometric Completion Window for Online Pre-Registrants

Applicants who begin pre-registration on the online CVR portal must complete physical biometric capture within 21 days.

2. Designated Biometric Capture Centres

Physical biometric capture for online pre-registrants is conducted at all 774 Local Government Area offices as well as State Headquarters.3. Treatment of Multiple or Duplicate Registrations

Where an applicant registers more than once during the CVR exercise, the subsequent duplicate registration shall be voided while the original valid registration record is retained.

4. Portal Maintenance Window

The online pre-registration portal performs automated backups every Sunday at 2:00 AM.
