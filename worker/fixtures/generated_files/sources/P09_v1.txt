1. Prohibition of PVC Collection by Proxy

A registered voter who is unable to attend in person may authorize a family member with a signed power of attorney to collect their PVC.

2. Ward-Level PVC Collection Closing Date

Ward-level devolution of PVC collection shall end on 20 January 2027, after which uncollected cards return to the LGA office.3. Operating Days and Hours for PVC Collection

PVC collection desks operate Monday to Friday between 9:00 AM and 3:00 PM and are closed on Saturdays and Sundays.

4. Daily Uncollected PVC Register

Collection officers shall log the serial numbers of collected PVCs at the close of each day.
