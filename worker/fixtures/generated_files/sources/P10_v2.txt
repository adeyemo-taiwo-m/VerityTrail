1. Observer Portal Submission Deadline

Domestic election observer groups must upload individual observer photographs and particulars by 18 February 2027.

2. Collation Centre Access Credentials

Accredited observers must present a QR-coded official INEC observer badge to enter ward collation centres; letters of introduction alone shall not grant entry.3. Voting Cubicle Secrecy and Photography

Photographing or filming marked ballot papers inside the voting cubicle is strictly prohibited for voters, journalists, and observers.

4. International Observer Briefing

A joint briefing for accredited observer missions will hold in Abuja two days before the poll.

5. Observer Field Report Submission

Accredited observer groups shall submit their preliminary observation report within 30 days after the election.

6. Emergency Incident Escalation Desk

Observers may report critical polling unit incidents through the dedicated observer situation room hotline.
