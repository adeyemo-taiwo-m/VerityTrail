1. Biometric Completion Window for Online Pre-Registrants

Applicants who begin pre-registration on the online CVR portal must complete physical biometric capture within 14 days.

2. Designated Biometric Capture Centres

Physical biometric capture for online pre-registrants is conducted only at the State Headquarters office.3. Treatment of Multiple or Duplicate Registrations

Where an applicant registers more than once during the CVR exercise, both the earlier and later registration records shall be cancelled.

4. Portal Maintenance Window

The online pre-registration portal performs automated backups every Sunday at midnight.
