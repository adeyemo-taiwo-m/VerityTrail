1. Continuous Voter Registration

Eligible citizens may register to vote at any registration centre until 15 January 2027.

2. Eligibility

A person must be at least 18 years old and a citizen of Nigeria to register.3. Collection of PVC

Registered voters must collect their Permanent Voter's Card within 60 days of notification.

4. Accreditation

Voters must present their PVC and a valid form of identification for accreditation before voting.

5. Transfer of registration

Voters who have moved may apply for transfer of registration during the registration window.
