1. Polling Unit Opening and Closing Hours

Accreditation and voting at all polling units shall close promptly at 2:30 PM, provided voters already in the queue at 2:30 PM shall be allowed to vote.

2. Biometric Authentication on BVAS

Where fingerprint authentication fails on the BVAS device, the Presiding Officer shall verify the voter strictly by BVAS facial authentication without issuing any Incident Form.3. Voter Registration Eligibility

To register as a voter, an applicant must be at least 18 years old and a citizen of Nigeria without any minimum ward residency duration requirement.

4. Presiding Officer Logbook

The Presiding Officer shall record the serial number and battery level of the BVAS device before opening the poll.
