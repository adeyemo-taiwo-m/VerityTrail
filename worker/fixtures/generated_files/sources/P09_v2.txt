1. Prohibition of PVC Collection by Proxy

Every registered voter must appear in person to collect their PVC; collection by proxy or power of attorney is strictly prohibited.

2. Ward-Level PVC Collection Closing Date

Ward-level devolution of PVC collection shall end on 5 February 2027, after which uncollected cards return to the LGA office.3. Operating Days and Hours for PVC Collection

PVC collection desks operate Monday to Sunday including Saturdays and Sundays between 8:30 AM and 5:00 PM.

4. Daily Uncollected PVC Register

Collection officers shall log and sign the serial numbers of collected PVCs at the close of each day.
