1. Public Posting of Form EC8A at Polling Unit

After counting votes, the Presiding Officer may optionally paste a copy of Form EC8A at the polling unit before leaving for the ward collation centre.

2. Electronic Transmission of Result Sheets

The Presiding Officer shall transmit the scanned Form EC8A result sheet only after arriving at the Ward Collation Centre.3. Statutory Definition of Over-Voting

Over-voting occurs where total votes cast at a polling unit exceed the total number of registered voters on the manual register.

4. Packaging of Tamper-Evident Envelopes

Used ballot counterfoils shall be sealed inside envelope EC50A before departure.
