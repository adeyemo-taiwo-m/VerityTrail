1. Deployment of Braille Ballot Guides

Braille ballot guides and magnifying sheets shall be deployed to all polling units nationwide with at least 1 registered visually impaired voter.

2. Priority Queue Access

Pregnant women, nursing mothers, elderly citizens, and persons with disabilities shall be granted immediate priority queue access from 8:30 AM.3. Eligibility of Assisting Companions

A voter with a physical disability who requires assistance in the voting cubicle must be assisted by a trusted family member of their choice and shall not be assisted by a party agent or poll official.

4. Form EC40H Disability Recording

Presiding Officers shall record disaggregated disability participation counts on Form EC40H and transmit the summary.
