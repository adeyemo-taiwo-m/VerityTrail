1. Observer Portal Submission Deadline

Domestic election observer groups must upload individual observer photographs and particulars by 5 February 2027.

2. Collation Centre Access Credentials

Accredited observers may enter ward collation centres using either an official INEC observer badge or a letter of introduction from their organization.3. Voting Cubicle Secrecy and Photography

Accredited journalists and voters are permitted to photograph marked ballot papers inside the voting cubicle for personal verification.

4. International Observer Briefing

A joint briefing for accredited observer missions will hold in Abuja three days before the poll.

5. Observer Field Report Submission

Accredited observer groups shall submit their preliminary observation report within 14 days after the election.
