1. Deployment of Braille Ballot Guides

Braille ballot guides shall be deployed only to urban polling units with at least 20 registered visually impaired voters.

2. Priority Queue Access

Pregnant women, nursing mothers, and persons with disabilities may join the general queue or request priority access after 11:00 AM.3. Eligibility of Assisting Companions

A voter with a physical disability who requires assistance in the voting cubicle may be assisted by any person including a party agent.

4. Form EC40H Disability Recording

Presiding Officers shall record disaggregated disability participation counts on Form EC40H.
