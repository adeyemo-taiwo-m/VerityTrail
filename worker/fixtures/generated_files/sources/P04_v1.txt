1. IDP Voter Revalidation Deadline

Internally displaced persons in designated camps must complete voter record revalidation by 10 October 2026.

2. Accreditation Documents at IDP Polling Centres

Displaced voters without a physical PVC may be accredited using a camp registration slip signed by the camp manager.3. Scope of Elections for Out-of-State IDP Camps

Displaced voters residing in camps outside their home state are eligible to vote in Presidential and Governorship elections.

4. Security Escort for IDP Materials

Sensitive materials for IDP centres shall be accompanied by unarmed civil defence personnel.
