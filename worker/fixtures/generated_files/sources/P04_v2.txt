1. IDP Voter Revalidation Deadline

Internally displaced persons in designated camps must complete voter record revalidation by 25 November 2026.

2. Accreditation Documents at IDP Polling Centres

Displaced voters at IDP polling centres must present a reissued Permanent Voter's Card or be verified on the BVAS camp register; camp registration slips shall not be accepted.3. Scope of Elections for Out-of-State IDP Camps

Displaced voters residing in camps outside their home state are eligible to vote only in the Presidential election.

4. Security Escort for IDP Materials

Sensitive materials for IDP centres shall be accompanied by inter-agency electoral security personnel.
